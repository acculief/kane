Kane

A bell, now and then.

Kane is a mindfulness bell. Through the day a bell rings at gentle, unpredictable moments: stop, take three breaths, and come back to what you were doing. When you sit, Kane keeps time with a starting bell, interval bells and an ending bell, and they ring even with the screen locked.

The bowls and bells are synthesized from the physics of struck metal: a singing bowl, a Japanese rin, a temple bell, a crystal bowl, tingsha and a wood block. No ads, no account, no subscription. Kane Pro is a single purchase.

Bells don't ring?

Notification bells follow the ring/silent switch and Focus. Turn the switch to ring, or allow Kane in your Focus settings. iOS lets an app plan only a few days of bells ahead, so open Kane every few days to keep them coming.

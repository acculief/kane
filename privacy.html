Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What Kane collects

Nothing personal. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics. Your settings and your log of sits are stored only on your device. Bells are scheduled as local notifications on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

The one-time Kane Pro purchase is handled by Apple and validated with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase record only — never your name or email.
